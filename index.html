Faelo Privacy Policy Last updated: May 2026 Faelo is a private social network that helps friends share upcoming plans and discover when their lives are about to overlap. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights over it. Who we are Faelo is owned and operated by Faelo Ltd ("we", "us", "our"). If you have questions about this policy, contact us at: enquiries.faelo@outlook.com What data we collect Data you give us directly Account information — your name, profile photo, and email address (or Apple ID) when you sign up Posts and plans — trips, events, happenings, and other content you share on Faelo Messages — direct messages you send to friends within the app Photos — images you choose to upload to your profile or scrapbook Data we collect automatically Device information — device type, operating system version, and app version Crash and diagnostic data — anonymised crash reports to help us fix bugs (via Firebase Crashlytics) Usage data — how you interact with the app, to help us improve it Data from your device (with your permission) Contacts — if you grant permission, we access your contacts to help you find friends already on Faelo. We do not store your full contacts list on our servers. Location — if you grant permission, we use your location to suggest nearby events and cities. You can disable this at any time in your device settings. Photos and camera — if you grant permission, to let you upload images to your profile and scrapbook How we use your data We use your data to: Create and manage your account Power the overlap engine — surfacing when your plans coincide with friends' Send you push notifications about friend activity and overlaps (you can disable these in settings) Improve the app through crash reporting and usage analysis Respond to support requests We do not: Sell your data to third parties Use your data for advertising Share your future plans with anyone outside your confirmed friends on Faelo Who we share data with Firebase (Google) — we use Firebase for authentication, database, file storage, push notifications, and crash reporting. Your data is stored on Firebase infrastructure. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy We do not share your personal data with any other third parties, except where required by law. Where your data is stored Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers. Firebase infrastructure is located in the United States and European Union. If you are based in the UK or EU, your data may be transferred internationally. Firebase complies with standard contractual clauses for such transfers. How long we keep your data We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data, posts, and messages will be permanently deleted within 30 days. Your rights If you are based in the UK or EU, you have the right to: Access the data we hold about you Correct inaccurate data Delete your account and all associated data Object to how we process your data Export your data in a portable format To exercise any of these rights, contact us at enquiries.faelo@outlook.com Children's privacy Faelo is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it promptly. Changes to this policy If we make significant changes to this policy, we will notify you via the app or email before the changes take effect. The date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent update.
